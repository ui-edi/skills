# Antarium landing page – Brief

**Authored under creative delegation** ("leg mal los"), based on Edi's itch.io text and patch notes for Antarium 1.1.

1. **What and for whom:** A calm, cozy ant colony sim in the browser. For people who want something to watch and check in on, not something to win.
2. **Believe at the end:** "There is a tiny living world behind the glass, and it's worth checking in on."
3. **Action:** Play in browser (free, pay what you want) → https://ui-edi.itch.io/antarium
4. **Vibe:** calm, earthy, nature documentary at dusk, a terrarium on a windowsill. References: a nature film's slow opening shot, an ant farm on a school desk, rain on a window.
5. **The moment:** "It's the site where scrolling digs you down through the earth into the ant nest."
6. **Assets:** none supplied for this test → everything drawn in SVG/CSS, pixel-ish shapes in the game's earthy palette.

## Grammar

**One world (lean) with filmic beats.** The game itself is a cut through the earth, so the page is one: you start above ground and the peak takes you underground. Chapters or a gallery would hide that single idea.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Cause | Device | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Meadow | wonder | layered meadow at dusk, title between the hills and the grass | parallax | 100vh + flow |
| 2 Slow down | relief | three short lines appear one by one on a still frame | pin + cues | 240vh |
| 3 Dig (peak) | discovery | scrolling digs the tunnel down, chambers light up, depth counter | custom (signature) | 420vh |
| 4 Weather | awe | day wipes into a stormy night with rain | reveal | 200vh |
| 5 Your way | choice | play styles slide past sideways | pan | 280vh |
| 6 Facts | trust | real numbers and features fade in | count + in | flow |
| 7 Close | calm, settled | night meadow, one action, holds | flow (static close) | 100vh |

**Peak:** Act 3, longest act, after the quietest act (2).
**Signature move:** the tunnel is drawn by your scroll, ants walk the part you've dug, chambers appear when you reach them, and a depth gauge counts the centimetres.
