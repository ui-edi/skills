# Klartext final check

Go through this before returning an edit or a draft. Fix every failure.

## Meaning and voice

- [ ] Point, claims, numbers, examples and opinions are unchanged (unless the user asked for a content change).
- [ ] Nothing was invented: no facts, quotes, sources, experiences, certainty.
- [ ] The writer's words, humor, dialect, du/Sie choice and rough edges are still there.
- [ ] Good sentences were left alone; the amount of editing matches the amount of slop.
- [ ] Swiss writers still have "ss" and «…».

## Clarity

- [ ] The text gets to the point without a warm-up.
- [ ] Abstract labels were replaced by facts, actions or consequences, or removed.
- [ ] German noun chains were turned back into verbs where it reads better.
- [ ] One thing has one name throughout.
- [ ] Sentence rhythm varies with the content, not by template.

## Patterns

- [ ] No staged contrasts, tee-ups, colon reveals, self-answered questions or fragment stacks left, including reworded versions.
- [ ] No importance labels, reaction instructions, vague authority or fake secrecy.
- [ ] No inflated buzzwords or empty adverbs that carry no meaning.
- [ ] No chatbot residue at start or end.
- [ ] No recap ending or fake-profound closing line.
- [ ] No long dash used as default glue; formatting helps navigation only.

## Read aloud

- [ ] It sounds natural said out loud to a sharp friend.
- [ ] The writer would recognize it as their own.
- [ ] It ends on a concrete point, result or next step.
- [ ] The answer contains the text and the short change list, nothing else.

For an audit: every finding has a pattern name, a short quote and a one-line fix. No score, no verdict on authorship.
